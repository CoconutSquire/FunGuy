## Entry format

Use a concise entry for each coherent work batch. Related changes may be grouped, but every substantive change or finding must be recorded.

- **Date / subject:** When and what the work concerns.
- **Status:** Implemented, observed, proposed, blocked or superseded; distinguish mixed statuses explicitly.
- **Affected files/systems:** Paths or components involved.
- **Changes/findings and rationale:** What changed or was learned, with the reason and supporting evidence.
- **Production-path impact:** Architectural alignment, milestone progress, dependencies, or a justified deviation and migration/replacement plan.
- **Validation:** Checks actually performed, their results, and relevant checks not performed.
- **Remaining work:** Unresolved issues, follow-up tasks and decisions needed; use “None” when appropriate.
